INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY

Deviation is a reason to review, not proof of misconduct.

Learning, collaboration, frameworks and task changes can all alter coding behaviour. The public benchmark is prototype-scale. The calibrated false-positive rate is shown above; the stricter operating point sends ambiguous cases to Uncertain and therefore reduces automatic coverage.
